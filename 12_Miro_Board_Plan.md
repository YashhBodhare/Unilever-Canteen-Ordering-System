# Miro Board Plan

This board layout supports a validation workshop. The board has not been used to record a completed workshop.

## Board frames

1. **Purpose and outcomes**: problem statement, goals, measures, participants, and decision rights.
2. **Stakeholder map**: employees, canteen manager, kitchen, delivery, HR/payroll, IT, management, product, and test roles. Confirm influence and interest.
3. **Current lunch journey**: travel, queue, menu choice, payment, seating, eating, return to workstation. Add observed waits and evidence source.
4. **Pain points and causes**: queue, limited seating, unavailable items, walk-in demand, preparation uncertainty, delivery and payment issues. Mark each cause as observed, reported, or hypothesis.
5. **Future order journey**: publish menu, sign in, select, confirm, cutoff, prepare, assign, deliver, close, feedback, reports.
6. **Scope boundary**: employee ordering, menu, kitchen summary, delivery, feedback, reports; park inventory, vendors, refunds, notifications, dietary features, and mobile support until decided.
7. **Requirements and priorities**: review BRD/FRD IDs and proposed MoSCoW values.
8. **Risks and decisions**: payroll model, cutoff, cancellation, device scope, delivery capacity, metric definitions.
9. **Actions and owners**: decision, evidence needed, owner, due date, status.

## Workshop agenda (90 minutes)

| Time | Topic | Output |
|---:|---|---|
| 0-10 min | Confirm purpose and outcomes | Agreed workshop goals |
| 10-25 min | Walk through current lunch process | Validated As-Is map and pain points |
| 25-45 min | Review future ordering and fulfilment | To-Be process with exception paths |
| 45-60 min | Confirm scope and business rules | In-scope/out-of-scope decisions |
| 60-75 min | Prioritize requirements | Draft MoSCoW list |
| 75-90 min | Review open questions and assign actions | Decision log and owners |
